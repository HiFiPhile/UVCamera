# Changelog

### Unreleased

First release.

* Turns a rooted phone (KernelSU or Magisk) with a UVC gadget capable
  kernel into a USB webcam: MJPEG up to 1080p30 and YUYV, alongside ADB and
  MTP.
* Front or back camera, maximum resolution, orientation (0/90/180/270°) and
  mirroring, all changeable while streaming.
* Host controls: brightness, zoom, focus, white balance and power line
  frequency.
* Keeps working with the screen off and across USB mode changes.
